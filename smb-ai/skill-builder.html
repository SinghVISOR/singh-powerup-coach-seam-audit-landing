The ladder

How far does your AI scale?

There are four levels of automation. Most owners live on level 1 forever. Skill Builder walks you up the ladder as your needs grow — each level does more of the work without more of your time.

Level 1

Skill

One saved task. "Draft the quote," "reply to this review," "triage the ticket." One click, one result.
Under the hood: a single instruction file SKILL.md Claude follows in one session.
Level 2

Specialist

A helper that goes and does a job. "Research this lead and report back." It works on its own, then hands you a summary.
Under the hood: a sub-agent that runs isolated and returns a summary.
Level 3

Team

Several specialists sharing one to-do list. Month-end close: a bookkeeper, a collections helper, and a report writer working together.
Under the hood: peer agents sharing a task list.
Level 4

Auto-Workflow

Describe the outcome, the system fans it out. "Onboard this new client" fires 8 steps at once across many helpers.
Under the hood: a workflow script that distributes work to many agents.

You don't need to know any of the technical detail. You describe the outcome — we build the level that fits, and move you up when it pays off.

Plain english

What a Skill actually is

A Skill is an SOP for your AI employee — a folder with a plain-language description of a task and how you like it done. Two things make it powerful for a small business:

It only loads what it needs

A Skill pulls in its detailed steps only when the task actually comes up. That keeps your AI fast and your per-use cost low — it isn't dragging every SOP around all day.

It comes two ways

Pre-built packs exist for sales, legal, data and marketing — thousands of ready Skills you install in one click. Or we build custom Skills from your business so they match how you work.
