How every specialist works

What a Mark the Line card looks like

Every specialist ships with one. It puts the HUMAN Framework inside each specialist, so you always know what the AI does and what stays with you.

Mark the Line · Invoice Chaser

The AI's job, and yours

  • H · Halt: before any send, check whether this client is one of your top five. If so, it comes to you first.
  • U · Understand: the real goal is getting paid and keeping the client, not just sending reminders.
  • M · Mark the Line: the AI lists overdue invoices and drafts reminders. You approve every send.
  • A · Apply Leverage: run it every Monday and let it do the chasing you put off.
  • N · Never Outsource: payment plans, write-offs and hard conversations stay with you.
